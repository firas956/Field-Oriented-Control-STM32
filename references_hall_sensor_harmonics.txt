REFERENCES - HALL SENSOR POSITION ERROR AND CURRENT HARMONICS IN PMSM FOC
================================================================================
Topic : Why misplaced Hall-effect sensors produce a 3rd harmonic in the phase
        current spectrum of a vector-controlled PMSM.
Project: FOC_IMP - STM32F446RE / PMSM / Hall sensors + tracking PLL
Compiled: 2026-07-23

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. REFERENCE-FRAME THEORY  (the h <-> h+/-1 harmonic mapping)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The canonical derivation of how a component at harmonic h in the rotating (dq)
frame appears at harmonics h+/-1 in the stationary (abc) frame. This is the
foundation of the whole argument.

[1] P. C. Krause, O. Wasynczuk, S. D. Sudhoff, S. Pekarek,
    "Analysis of Electric Machinery and Drive Systems", 3rd ed.,
    Wiley-IEEE Press, 2013.
    -> See the "Reference Frame Theory" chapter.

[2] D. W. Novotny, T. A. Lipo,
    "Vector Control and Dynamics of AC Drives",
    Oxford University Press, 1996.
    -> Same material, more drive-oriented.

[3] S.-K. Sul,
    "Control of Electric Machine Drive Systems",
    Wiley-IEEE Press, 2011.
    -> Most practical of the three. Covers dead-time effects, current
       measurement error and position sensor error as explicit non-ideality
       analyses. Best single book for this project.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. METHODOLOGICAL TEMPLATE  (same derivation, applied to current sensors)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The single most useful paper: it propagates a sensor non-ideality through the
Park transform and shows which harmonic results - exactly the derivation style
needed here, just for current sensing instead of position.

[4] D.-W. Chung, S.-K. Sul,
    "Analysis and compensation of current measurement error in
     vector-controlled AC motor drives",
    IEEE Transactions on Industry Applications, vol. 34, no. 2,
    pp. 340-345, 1998.
    URL: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/iel3/28/14535/00663477.pdf

    Key results:
      - OFFSET  error -> torque oscillates at 1x stator electrical frequency
      - SCALING error -> torque oscillates at 2x stator electrical frequency
    A 2nd harmonic in dq maps to the 1st and 3rd harmonics in abc, so a
    current-sensor GAIN MISMATCH is an independent cause of a 3rd harmonic
    with an identical signature. Directly relevant here because i_c is
    reconstructed as -(i_a + i_b), so only two channels are measured.

[5] "Diminution of Current-Measurement Error for Vector-Controlled AC
     Motor Drives",
    IEEE Transactions on Industry Applications (follow-up work).
    URL: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/1703718/


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. HALL SENSOR ASYMMETRY / MISPLACEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[6] N. Samoylenko, Q. Han, J. Jatskevich,
    "Dynamic Performance of Brushless DC Motors With Unbalanced Hall Sensors",
    IEEE Transactions on Energy Conversion, vol. 23, no. 3,
    pp. 752-763, 2008.
    URL: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/4537158/

    Explicitly drops the usual assumption that Hall sensors are ideally placed
    120 electrical degrees apart, and shows that misplaced sensors cause
    unbalanced inverter/motor phase operation which INCREASES THE LOW-FREQUENCY
    HARMONICS in torque ripple.
    CAVEAT: framed for six-step BLDC, not FOC with angle interpolation. The
    cause transfers directly; the propagation path in an FOC drive is through
    the Park transform rather than commutation timing.

[7] N. Samoylenko, Q. Han, J. Jatskevich,
    "Improving Dynamic Performance of Low-Precision Brushless DC Motors with
     Unbalanced Hall Sensors" (companion conference paper, mitigation).
    URL: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/4275987/

5. HOW TO CITE THE ARGUMENT  (important - calibration note)
================================================================================
No single source found states the full chain

    "Hall spacing asymmetry -> 2nd harmonic in dq -> 3rd harmonic in phase
     current"

in those words. It is a COMPOSITION of two individually rigorous, standard
results. For a report, present it as:

  (a) Derive the 180-degree periodicity of the sector-boundary errors directly
      (four lines - see the derivation summary below).
  (b) Invoke [1] (Krause) for the dq <-> abc harmonic mapping.
  (c) Cite [4] (Chung & Sul) as the precedent for this class of analysis, and
      [6] (Samoylenko et al.) for the misplaced-Hall cause.

Do NOT attribute the composed claim to a single reference.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DERIVATION SUMMARY (for step (a) above)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sensor A is high for 180 deg, giving edges at theta_A and theta_A+180.
Nominally B is at theta_A+120 / +300, and C at theta_A+240 / +60.
Adding a per-sensor placement error (both edges of a sensor shift together):

    0+dA    60+dC   120+dB   180+dA   240+dC   300+dB

The error sequence across the six sectors is  dA, dC, dB, dA, dC, dB  - it
repeats after THREE sectors = 180 electrical degrees. A 180-deg-periodic angle
error is a 2nd harmonic (plus 4th, 6th, ...). By the mapping in [1]:

    2nd harmonic in dq  ->  1st and 3rd harmonics in abc

Parity diagnostic:
    sensor SPACING / placement error  -> even angle-error harmonics (2,4,6)
                                      -> ODD current harmonics (1,3,5,7)
    DUTY asymmetry (threshold, hysteresis) -> odd angle-error harmonics (1,3,5)
                                      -> EVEN current harmonics (2,4,6)

Note: the 3rd harmonic produced this way is POSITIVE-SEQUENCE (the three phase
currents sum to zero), so it flows normally in a 3-wire star connection. Only
the ZERO-SEQUENCE (triplen) 3rd harmonic is blocked by the isolated neutral -
which is why the PMSM's own 3rd-harmonic back-EMF does not show up in the
current.
